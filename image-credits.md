# Image credits and licensing

All photography is staged in `assets-source/photos/` and optimised into
`apps/web/public/images/optimized/` as AVIF, WebP and JPEG at responsive widths.

> Before commercial launch, confirm every licence with the client. In particular,
> review the CC BY-SA attribution and share-alike obligations and validate the
> Vomtek-origin photography ownership.
